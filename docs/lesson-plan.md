# Lesson plan: Tsome Nebiyat 2019 (Nov 24, 2026 – Jan 6, 2027)

One short video a day, each a history lesson anchored on one Bible verse.
Three arcs build toward Genna: Ethiopia's place in the Bible, the prophets the fast is named for,
and the road to Bethlehem. Verses are from the World English Bible (public domain).

Status: ✅ published · 📝 written, on the site · ⬜ planned

## Weeks 2–5 · The prophets who saw it coming (Dec 1 – Dec 28)

One prophet or promise a day. Order to be set week by week; candidates:

- Isaiah 7:14: a virgin will conceive
- Isaiah 9:6: a child is born to us
- Micah 5:2: Bethlehem, small among the clans
- Jeremiah 23:5: a righteous Branch for David
- Daniel 1:12: Daniel's vegetable fast (why fasting looks the way it does)
- Jonah 3:5: Nineveh fasts (ties to Tsome Nenewe in February)
- Exodus 34:28 and 1 Kings 19:8: Moses' and Elijah's 40 days (why 40)
- Isaiah 53:7: the lamb led to the slaughter (what the Ethiopian official was reading)
- Hosea 11:1: out of Egypt I called my son
- Zechariah 9:9: your king comes to you, humble
- Malachi 3:1: I send my messenger
- Isaiah 11:1: a shoot from the stump of Jesse
- Abraham the Syrian and the moving mountain, Matthew 17:20 (check the tradition with Memhir Senay first)

## How each week runs

1. By Thursday, the next week's lessons are written and on the site, in Record Mode under /record/{date}.
2. Family or Memhir Senay review anything marked `"reviewed": false`.
3. Record the whole week in one sitting: OBS plus the iPhone voice memo, clap at the start of each take.
4. Post one a day, early evening, on TikTok, Reels, Shorts and Facebook.
