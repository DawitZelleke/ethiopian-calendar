# Lesson plan: Tsome Nebiyat 2019 (Nov 24, 2026 – Jan 6, 2027)

One short lesson a day, each anchored on one Bible verse and tied to the fast itself:
its names, the prophets who kept it, how it is kept, and the church's own seasons inside it
(Zemene Astemhro, then Zemene Sibket with its Sundays: Sibket, Birhan and Nolawi).
Verses are from the World English Bible (public domain).

Lessons are written a week ahead. Each one appears on the Lessons page on its own date
(`publishedLessons` in `web/src/lessons/index.ts`). Record Mode can open any date ahead of time.

Status: ✅ published · 📝 written, on the site · ⬜ planned

## How each week runs

1. By Thursday, the next week's lessons are written and on the site, in Record Mode under /record/{date}.
2. Family or Memhir Senay review anything marked `"reviewed": false`.
3. Record the whole week in one sitting.
4. Post one a day, early evening, on TikTok, Reels, Shorts and Facebook.
