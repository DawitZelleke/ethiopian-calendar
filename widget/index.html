Ethiopian Orthodox Calendar Widget

Fasts and feasts computed with Bahire Hasab, updated automatically every month.

Today banner

Month calendar

Add it to your site

Paste this into a WordPress "Custom HTML" block, or any web page:

<div class="ethiopian-calendar" data-view="month"></div>
<script src="https://dawitzelleke.github.io/ethiopian-calendar/widget.js" defer></script>

Options: data-view="today" for a one-line banner, data-lang="am" or "en" for one language, data-accent="#8a1c1c" for your colour.
